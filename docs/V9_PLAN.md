No art/code copied.
1. Preserve legacy saves, coins, existing duplicate rooms and ownership; no new duplicate blueprints including queued refits. Expand catalog to 28 unique rooms with authored interior assets, distinct service names, unlock cadence, staff dream jobs. 25 slots remain; later catalog choices intentionally compete for slots.
2. Timed single new construction queue, no earning/boarding during construction. First tutorial room upgrade immediate; subsequent restorations timed, batch quote cannot skip duration. Supply lots: three room-specific offers, first open to all, others unlocked by boarded crew. Finite stock measured in service seconds, paid replenishment and delivery; housing earns lower rent. Empty businesses retain low basic fares to avoid softlock. No monetization or daily punishment. Offline service capped as before; construction/delivery times bounded to fit cap. Rates and costs tuned using policy runs, not a claim of player retention.
3. Show status, timers, stock, staffing, unique catalog and actual art within existing inspector/shop. Use consistent existing design. Keep PWA and native icon identity.

Regression gates: original security/save/income/arrival/crossbrowser tests, adapt fixtures that assumed duplicate builds/immediate high-level upgrades to new contracts, add independent tests for boundaries, repeated transactions, segmented tick invariance, migrations, stock and unique catalog. Rerun 30-day and seeded annual policies with supply-aware decisions; report first-hour/day/week/month pacing and variety. Compare against V8 historical results with limitations. Physical-device/user tests separately.
5. Package and stage PR, not merge unverified candidate. Treat local saves as non-authoritative; no backend, secrets, network dependencies, or multiplayer. Revisit OWASP threats for changed save fields, timers, asset path allowlists, CSP, prototype keys, cache scope, bounded work and duplicate claims.
