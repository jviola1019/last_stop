# Economy balancing · Longline v5

This is a reproducible **deterministic simulation**, not real-player retention evidence. Playtime is not a currency. All values are in-game Coins, not money.

### Model and policies

Room upgrade cost for a car with base price `P` at current level `L` (before the purchase):

`round((55+0.2P) * 2.4^min(4,L−1) * 3^max(0,L−5))`.

Room revenue scales by `1.27^(L−1)`, with occupancy, satisfaction, resident bonuses and neighbor bonuses applied from the code. Rooms cap at level **20**. New carriages use `basePrice × 1.95^(cars−2)`; max **25** cars.

- **Active upper bound**: simulates someone operating 24/7, collecting every minute, buying at most one highest-income-per-price option per minute. Not representative of human use. After day 30 in `--long`, purchases are evaluated every 15 minutes instead of every minute (different policy).
- **Casual**: ten interactive minutes per day, one eight-hour offline income/service credit per 24h gap, at most one purchase per active minute, no station stops while absent.
- Neither policy triggers a new timetable/restart or buys cosmetics; purchases are optimized for earnings. Neither policy is a claim of optimal real-life satisfaction.
